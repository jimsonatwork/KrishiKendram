## Objective
Close confirmed Crop lifecycle races while keeping CropService the canonical owner of crop validation, defaults, duplicate protection, persistence, relationships and audit. Protect direct create, composite Intake create, update and restore from concurrent farm archive.

## Guardrails
- No database reset, destructive volume action, migration-history rewrite, or hard deletion of farm/user/history data.
- Authorization before Registry validation; central Registry only; preserve actor-attributed audit and temporal owner relationships.
- Historical Crop reads remain governed by authorization. Archived parent farms cannot receive new crops, crop updates, or restored child crops.
- Do not stage .cto-backup or historical untracked plan files.

## Findings and implementation
- Direct create, direct Intake create and transaction-aware Intake create share createCanonical; the outer farm context originally did not include archive state, and neither direct nor composite writes rechecked active parent state in the transaction.
- Crop update originally did not recheck active parent state in its mutation transaction.
- Crop restore could reactivate a crop under an archived parent farm.
- Added the shared assertFarmActive guard to all four write paths, added minimal parent deletedAt context checks before authorization/validation, and added race/archived-parent regression coverage.
